Privacy Policy

gta-shorts-bot is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses a single Google account (the owner's) with the YouTube upload scope so it can post videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it stores: OAuth credentials for the owner's own accounts, kept as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository. No data about any other person is collected, stored, or shared.

Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

To ask for any stored data to be deleted, email claygoldberg18@gmail.com.
